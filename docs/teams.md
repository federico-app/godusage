# Teams

Teams let you compare AI usage with friends. Create a team, share its invite link, and see who spends the most, on which providers and models. Teams are off until you sign in.

## Signing in

Open **Settings → Teams** and choose **Sign In with Apple**. Apple's sign-in page opens in Safari, whatever your default browser is; if Safari is already signed in to your Apple ID, you only confirm. Safari then asks to open GodUsage, which finishes the sign-in. A sign-in left unfinished for 10 minutes, or replaced by a new one, is dropped. The first sign-in creates your account. Your display name starts as the name Apple shares (or "GodUsage User") and you can change it in the same pane. It is what teammates see. GodUsage never receives or stores your email.

Once you're signed in, the pane shows one team at a time: pick it at the top (the same selection as the popover and the Teams window), next to **New Team…** and **Join Team…**. Below, the pages are **Members** (the team, its members, and its links), **Accounts** (which of this Mac's accounts count in it), **Plans** (owners only), and **You** (your name, this Mac's sharing, Show Rank in Menu Bar, iPhone and iPad, and the account actions). With no team yet, only **You** shows.

The sign-in stays on this Mac until you sign out, delete the account, or it goes unused for 180 days. Each Mac signs in on its own. Signing in on another Mac with the same Apple ID uses the same account, so your Macs add up to one person on the leaderboard.

## iPhone and iPad

The GodUsage iPhone app signs in to the same account by scanning a QR code from a Mac that is already signed in. Open **Settings → Teams → You → iPhone and iPad**, choose **Link iPhone or iPad…**, and scan the code with the app. A code works once and expires after three minutes; asking for a new one cancels the old one. The sheet closes on its own once the device links.

The section lists every linked device by name. **Unlink…** signs that device out. A linked device stays signed in when the Mac signs out, until it is unlinked, signs itself out, the account is deleted, or it goes unused for 180 days. A linked device cannot show codes for other devices. DEV builds link only to the DEV iPhone app, and production builds only to the production app.

Development builds (`com.montinovo.godusage.dev`) use a separate backend with separate accounts and teams, so testing never touches real leaderboards. See [Teams backend](teams-backend.md#environments).

## Teams and invites

- **Create a team** with **New Team…**. You are its owner.
- **Invite people** by copying the team's **Invite Link** on its **Members** page. Anyone with the link can join. Opening it shows the team name and an **Open in GodUsage** button. GodUsage then opens Settings → Teams and asks you to confirm. You can also paste a link into **Join Team…**.
- **Owners** can make a **New Link** (the old one stops working; members stay), share a public leaderboard, edit plans, **Rename…** the team, and delete it. From the **⋯** menu next to a member, they can **Make Owner…**, **Make Member** (for another owner), or **Remove…**. A team can have several owners, all with the same powers.
- **Count My Usage** (owners only, on by default): turn it off to run a team without being part of it. Your usage leaves its leaderboards, challenges, Hall of Fame, and plans, and plans can no longer cover you; you keep managing everything. The member list marks you **Not Counted**. A plan that covers only you must be changed first. Being made a member turns it back on.
- **Viewers** (set by owners): in a member's **…** menu, **Make Viewer** leaves that member's usage and accounts out of the team, just like Count My Usage off: off its leaderboards, challenges, Hall of Fame, and plans, and plans can no longer cover them. They still see everything. The member list marks them **Viewer**, and their Members and Accounts pages tell them. Only an owner can count them again (**Count Their Usage**). A plan that covers only them must be changed first.
- **Anyone** can leave. The last owner cannot; they make someone else an owner first, or delete the team.

A team holds up to 50 members, and you can be in up to 20 teams.

## What is shared

While you are signed in and in at least one team, this Mac uploads its own last 30 days of usage:

- daily tokens and spend per provider (Claude, Codex, Cursor, and the rest),
- the same per model.

Extra accounts of a provider are added into that provider; account ids never leave the Mac. An account you leave out of a team (see [Accounts counted](#accounts-counted)) is sent on its own, under an anonymous fingerprint, so the server can drop it from that team. Credentials, logs, prompts, and project names are never uploaded. Each Mac uploads only what it read itself. Disabled providers are left out.

The server keeps everything each Mac shared, so history grows beyond the app's 30-day window; each upload replaces only that Mac's last 30 days. After the first upload since launch, the Mac sends only the days that changed (usually just today), and the whole window again when a provider is turned off.

Uploads happen after each refresh (every 5 minutes) when your usage changed, at least every 15 minutes even when it didn't, and right away when you sign in, create or join a team, or change which providers are enabled. The server recomputes a board at most every few minutes (Today every 5, the others every 10), and never shows one older than 10 minutes, so a teammate's new usage reaches Today's board within about 10 minutes. Each upload replaces this Mac's previous one. The Account section shows when this Mac last shared. Each Mac uploads under a durable device ID kept in GodUsage's Application Support folder. If that ID cannot be read, the Mac does not upload, so it is never counted twice; the Account section says so and, when an older copy of the ID sits in the Keychain, offers **Recover Identity** to read it with your approval.

Your Macs are combined on the server. Local usage (Claude, Codex, Grok, and others) adds up across Macs. Cursor's usage is already account-wide, so only your most recently updated Mac counts.

**Shared accounts.** Cursor reports usage per account, not per person. When two or more members log into the same Cursor account, their usage is counted once: it is in the team total, the providers, By Day, and Top Models, but in no member's total. It shows as an unranked **Shared Cursor** row after the members, with who shares it, on the dashboard, in the Teams window, and on the web boards. To tell accounts apart, each Mac sends an anonymous fingerprint of its Cursor account (a one-way hash); the account id itself never leaves the Mac.

**Export My Data** (Settings → Teams → You) saves everything the service keeps about you as a JSON file. The service's [Terms](https://godusage-api.federico-c80.workers.dev/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://godusage-api.federico-c80.workers.dev/privacy) are linked at sign-in.

**Sign Out** removes this Mac's usage from your teams first, then ends the session. If removing it fails, you stay signed in and see the error. **Delete Account** removes your account, the usage all your Macs shared, and the teams you own.

### Accounts Counted

Settings → Teams → Accounts lists, for the selected team, every account this Mac shares (each Claude or Codex login, Cursor, and the other providers). All of them count by default. Turn one off, like a personal plan next to the work one, to leave it out of that team only: its spend, tokens, and models leave the team's leaderboards, challenges, Hall of Fame, and ⚡, and its quota leaves the team's plans. Your other teams still count it. Teammates see only your smaller total, never the left-out account. The change uploads at once and shows on the team's boards right away.

The choice is kept on this Mac, per team. If an account is on several Macs, turn it off on each one. It applies to the last 30 days, which every upload resends; older days stay as they were shared.

## Leaderboards

Ranges are Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, and Year. The server keeps every day each Mac uploaded, so the Year range fills in from the day you start sharing.

**On the dashboard.** Once you're signed in and in a team, a Team section sits right under Total Spend, above the provider cards. It shows the selected team's ranking. The period follows the Total Spend card's switcher (Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, 30 Days; Year is in the Teams window), and the metric follows its menu: Cost, Cost/MTok (what each member pays per million tokens, highest first, without movement arrows), or Tokens. Each member's bar is split by provider. Each row keeps the top line for the rank, the name, and the amount; the line under the bar carries the status on the left and the marks on the right (movement, ⚡, 👑, 🏆, reaction counts), which never get cut off. Click a member to see, in words, everything their row shows (their place and movement, their last hour and why it earned ⚡, their crowns, who reacted, when they last synced and from which version), then their providers and top models. Only the top 2 members show at first; the caret under them shows the rest. **Advanced Stats** at the bottom opens the Teams window. With more than one team, pick one from the team name. The section reloads each time you open the popover and every 2 minutes while it stays open; the Teams window also reloads every 2 minutes while open. The refresh button next to the team name sends this Mac's usage now and reloads the board. If this Mac's last upload failed, the section says why under the board (Settings → Teams shows it too). If the teams server has used up its daily database allowance, boards keep their last numbers and say that updates are paused until midnight UTC. Press **⌘T**, or choose **Team** in the gear menu, to open the Teams window directly.

**Customize → Team** (shown while you're in a team) changes how the section looks: turn it off on the dashboard, pick how many members show before the caret (Top 2, Top 3, Top 5, or All), and show or hide the team projection and the challenges.

**In the Teams window** (the popover's chart button, or **Open Leaderboards** in Settings → Teams):

- the ranking (members with the same value share a rank), each bar split by provider,
- **Who Uses What**: each member's usage, split by provider,
- **By Day**: the team's usage per day, split by provider; hover a day for its numbers,
- **By Model**: the team's usage per day, week, or month, split by the period's eight largest models (the rest are summed as Other); hover a bar for its numbers. A year starts by month, shorter periods by day,
- **Top Models**: the team's most used models. Each model takes its provider's color, in a different shade for each model of the same provider (the largest keeps the plain color), in both By Model and Top Models.

The gear button in its toolbar opens Settings → Teams.

**Plans.** Team owners list the subscriptions the team pays for in Settings → Teams → Plans: provider (just the provider, like Claude, never one of your local accounts: a plan covers the team members you pick, whichever login they use), name, monthly cost, the day of the month it renews (29–31 fall on the last day of shorter months), and who it **Covers**: **Everyone** (the default, including members who join later) or the members picked from the list. Every member sees the **Plans** tab in the Teams window. For each plan it shows the current billing cycle (last renewal to the day before the next), the covered members' usage of that provider in the cycle at API prices, the projection to the end of the cycle at the current pace, and the value per dollar (projected value ÷ what the cycle costs). Forecasts count only the team's **working days**, Monday to Friday by default; the owner changes them in Settings → Teams → Plans. Usage on other days counts toward the value, but sets no pace: the projection adds the cycle's remaining working days at the value per working day so far, and the open quota window is paced on its working time. A plan that covers only some members lists them. For Claude, Codex, and Cursor it also shows the **quota used**: how much of their weekly limit (Claude, Codex) or the cycle's included usage (Cursor) the plan's accounts use in the cycle, averaged per account, and what the unused part costs. It counts accounts, not members, since one account can be shared, so it is shown per plan and never per person. Each Mac records the highest share it sees of each window before it resets, and the server keeps each week's and each session's readings for 13 months, so past weeks can be compared; a week the Mac last read more than 6 hours before its reset shows as estimated, because its share may have grown after. The open window counts at its pace to the reset. The header sums the quota used across plans (weighted by cost) and the unused dollars. For Claude and Codex, each plan also shows **Per Day**: the share of the weekly limit its accounts use each day over the last seven days, averaged per account, against the pace that uses exactly the week in the working days (100% ÷ working days per week, 20% by default). Each Mac records the share it sees at the end of each day, so a day's use is the growth since the day before; days no Mac read the window are spread evenly and marked with ~. Days off show in grey and, like today (not over yet), stay out of the average. Next to it, **Sessions** shows the cycle's 5-hour sessions: how many hit the limit, one square per session (darker the more of it was used, outlined when it hit the limit), and the average peak. Under them, **Weekly History** (collapsed until opened) lists the plan's last 12 weekly windows, reset to reset like the provider's own weekly limit, newest first: one cell per day of the window with the share of the weekly limit used that day (darker the more, ~ when estimated, a dot when no Mac read it, "off" on days off with no use), the window's share of the limit as the provider reported it ("limit reached" at 100%), and its sessions (how many hit the limit, average peak). The columns are named after the newest window's days; a window that a reset moved names its own days. When a plan has several accounts, the account that resets first sets the weeks and each week says how many accounts it averages. Each account is named by a one-way hash of its account id (Claude: the account and its organization, from Claude Code's sign-in; Codex: the ChatGPT user and account), so one account read on several Macs counts once, at the highest share any of them saw. A Mac that can't read the account id (an older GodUsage, or no Claude Code or Codex sign-in) names the account by itself, so the same account on several Macs would count once per Mac; the plan then warns that its quota, Per Day, and Sessions may be off. Usage itself is never merged this way: each member's Mac adds the usage in its own logs. Plans on pace to use less than half their quota are flagged as underused; plans without quota data (other providers, or before any covered Mac reports one) are flagged when projected under 1×. Members a plan doesn't cover, like someone paying for their own Cursor, don't count toward its value. Usage counts like the leaderboard, shared accounts once; a shared account counts for a plan when anyone who shares it is covered. Plans of one provider that cover the same usage split it by cost. An underused plan that renews within 7 days also shows as a [suggestion](dashboard.md#suggestions) on the dashboard of the members it covers who use its provider, whichever team they have selected.

**Plan changes.** **Change Plan…** on a plan marks a downgrade or upgrade: the new name, the new monthly price, and the **Effective** day. It defaults to the next renewal, and can be any later day or a past one (back to the day after the plan's last change). Saving it saves the plans. The plan keeps its price history: until the day, it has its old name and price; from the day, the new ones. A billing cycle with a change in it costs each price for the days it applies (monthly price ÷ the cycle's days, per day), and each day's usage counts against the price in effect that day, so the Plans tab shows the cycle's real cost and value per dollar. Under the plan, in Settings and in the Plans tab for every member, changes in the current cycle or later show as "↓ Downgrade to Claude Max 100 · $100/mo from Nov 30 (next renewal)" (↑ when the price goes up), or "↓ Downgraded to … on Oct 12" once the day has come. Owners can **Cancel Change** on any of them; the plan then keeps the terms it had before.

**Detected plans.** Each Mac sends, with its usage, the plan GodUsage detects for each provider (the plan name a provider card shows, like "Max 20x"). In Settings, **Detected As** links a team plan to one of the plans detected on its covered members' Macs; when exactly one is detected and the plan has no link, it's filled in for you (save to keep it). When a covered member's Mac then detects another plan, owners see on that plan: "⚠ Detected Pro on Bea's Mac. This plan says Max 20x ($200/mo)." **Update Payment…** opens the change editor with the detected plan as the name and the day it was first detected (on Bea's Mac) as the effective day; enter the new price and save, and the plan links to the new detected plan. **Ignore** stops flagging that detected plan for this plan. A detected plan that a change in the current cycle or later already names is not flagged. Only owners see detected plans and warnings; members see the plan's changes.

**Last update.** Each member shows when their Macs last uploaded, on the dashboard, in the Teams window, and on the web boards: **Updated 5m ago** (or **just now**, **3h ago**) within the last 24 hours, then **Not synced for N days** with a warning icon, so their numbers aren't read as no usage. In the app, the GodUsage version follows only when it is older than the newest one in the team (**· v1.0.8, outdated**), so you can tell who is behind on updates; the member's detail always shows it. Uploads from apps before 1.0.8 carry no version. Members who never synced show nothing.

**Accelerating.** A member spending fast right now gets one to three **⚡**, and their status line shows what they spent in the last hour (**+$12.40/h**) instead of the last update. Each rule met adds a ⚡, once the last hour reaches $1: at least $5 in the last hour; at least twice their typical active hour of the previous seven days; and the most in the team in the last hour. The member's detail says which rules they met. It counts what each upload added, so it follows the Macs' upload pace (a few seconds after usage changes); a Mac's first upload never counts, and a shared account counts for no one. Boards recompute it at most once a minute.

**Team life.** Around the ranking:

- **👑** marks today's top spender and **🏆** last month's champion; hover either for its meaning (the **Hall of Fame** in the Teams window lists every month's champion; a month counts once it's over).
- **Reactions:** give a teammate 🔥, 👏, or 🤡, one of each per day; click again to take it back. Counts start clean every day at midnight UTC. A member's detail lists who gave each one today. On the dashboard's team section, click a member to react; the Teams window has the buttons on each row.
- **Projection:** "Team on pace for $X in October" stretches this month's spend so far over the whole month, for the team and for each member.
- **This Week's Limit:** above the ranking, each Claude or Codex plan with an open weekly limit shows its week so far, from the last reset to the next: one bar per day of use, today's use against **today's budget**, and the working days left drawn at that budget, with the full-pace line. The budget is what was left of the weekly limit at the start of today, spread evenly over today and the working days until the reset, so it shrinks after a heavy day and grows after a light one. With Live Pace on, it is spread over working hours instead (see below). The line under it says how much more today stays on budget (or how far over it is) and when the limit resets; with three plans or more it is left out. A plan whose week mixes several accounts says how many, and uses the one that resets first. The strip shows the selected team's plans, plus the plans that cover you in your other teams, each named with its team, so picking a big team for its leaderboard still shows your own weekly limit. A subscription set up as a plan in two teams shows once (the selected team's copy first): plans count as the same when they're for the same provider and either name the same plan (the detected plan when both have one, otherwise the name) or their weekly limits reset at the same moment. Your other teams' plans reload at most every 30 minutes. Turn it off in Customize → Team → **Weekly Limit Budget**.
- **Live Pace:** with it on (Customize → Team → **Live Pace**, under Weekly Limit Budget; on by default), each plan's row in This Week's Limit says how you're doing right now against today's budget. Today's budget is then spread over **working hours** instead of working days: what was left of the weekly limit at the start of today, times today's share of the working hours until the reset. The working hours are those of everyone who uses the plan's accounts, combined: a shared account counts each person's hours, in each person's own time zone, and a break counts only when everyone is on it. The row shows:
  - one figure (hover it for what it means): how far ahead of (**+44 min**, orange) or behind (**−1h 10m**, green) where today's use should be at this point of the working day, measured in working time, or **On Pace** within five minutes. Over today's budget it shows how far over (**+5.9%**); outside the hours it shows **Starts 9:00**, **Day Done**, or **Day Off**;
  - under it, today's use against the budget and the one thing to know next: when the budget runs out at the current pace, how much is left today, or how the following days change;
  - the week's strip with a tick in today's bar where today's use should be now. The working days left until the reset share what today leaves: going over today's budget, or heading over it at the current pace, lowers them at once (their bars turn orange), and a day that ends under budget raises them;
  - **Details** (collapsed): the current pace and the pace that lasts until the end of the working day (in % of the weekly limit per hour), today's working hours and who else's hours count, and a chart of today: the ideal pace, the use so far, and where the current pace leads.

  The pace is the growth over the last working hour, from the readings every covered Mac sends; without a reading that old it is today's average. In the first working hour of the day there is no color, and it's too early for alerts. The figure turns orange when you're ahead of pace and the current pace ends today over 110% of the budget (back once it's under 100%), and green when you're behind and it ends under 70% (back over 80%), so it doesn't flicker at a threshold. When the two disagree, like ahead now but slowing down, it stays uncolored; its tooltip says where the current pace leads. When one provider's plan is running hot (orange or over budget) and another provider's plan has at least 5% of today's budget left, that plan's row says **use this now**. Plans from apps or servers before Live Pace show the strip as before. Your other teams' plans reload every 5 minutes with Live Pace on.
- **Working Hours** (Settings → Teams → Members): each member's working days and hours, with an optional break, in the time zone of their Mac (it follows the Mac, so working abroad moves the hours with you). Everyone sets their own; owners set anyone's in their team. The hours are the person's, so every team they're in uses them. A member who never set theirs works the team's working days, 9:00 to 18:00. **Use Team Default** goes back to that.
- **Challenges** (Teams window, **New Challenge**): any member starts one for 7, 14, or 30 days: **Lowest Spend** (among members who spend something), **Most Models**, **Most Tokens**, or **Best Efficiency** (with at least 100K tokens). Standings update live; when it ends, the leader wins. The creator or an owner can cancel it. Active challenges also show under the dashboard's team ranking.

Every ranking except Year shows each member's movement since the period before: ▲ places gained, ▼ places lost, or **New** for someone who had no usage then. Each member also shows **Efficiency**, what they pay per million tokens (lower means cheaper models or more cache use); the Teams window charts it for the whole team.

**Compare** (Teams window) puts two members side by side: totals, rank, efficiency, each provider as mirrored bars, their top models, and their days as a solid and a dashed line. It starts with you and the next member; pick anyone from either name.

**Compare → Plan Weeks** puts two weekly windows of Claude or Codex plans side by side, from the same team or from two teams you're in (blue and orange, each with its team, plan, and week). It shows each day's share of the weekly limit by position in the window (each window starts at its own reset, so the days are named on each side), and a table with the weekly limit used, whether it was reached, the busiest day, sessions at the limit, average session peak, and accounts. A line under it says which used more of its limit, notes a week still running, and warns when the two plans differ, since the shares are then of different limits. With one team, the right side starts on the week before.

**Share Wrapped** (Teams window) copies an image of your standing in the team over the last 30 days or 12 months: your rank, spend, tokens, efficiency, top provider and model, and the team's top five.

**Show Rank in Menu Bar** (Settings → Teams → You) adds your rank, your share of the team's spend, and your spend today in the selected team to the menu bar. See [Menu bar](menu-bar.md#team-rank).

Providers have the same color everywhere: the popover's Cost ring, the dashboard's Team section, the Teams window, and the web boards (Claude is terracotta, Codex green, Cursor black, or white in dark mode). Every chart also has a legend and value labels, so no number depends on color alone.

The popover, the Teams window, and Settings → Teams each keep their own team. Switching team in the Teams window or in Settings leaves the popover and the menu bar on theirs. **Advanced Stats** and **Open Plans** open the Teams window on the popover's team, and **Open Leaderboards** on the team Settings shows. Until you pick one there, or when you leave it, the Teams window and Settings show the popover's team.

Days are calendar days on each Mac. Refresh uploads this Mac's latest usage and reloads.

Every member can open the team's **Web Leaderboard** in a browser. It shows the same board after you sign in with your Apple ID, and only to members.

An owner can also turn on **Public Leaderboard** for a team. That gives a read-only web page anyone with its link can open without signing in. It shows display names, ranks, spend or tokens by provider, and top models. Turning it off makes the link stop working.

## Notifications

Six team alerts in **Settings → Notifications**, all on by default:

- **Team Overtakes**: when a teammate passes you by spend over the last 7 days. The first check after you turn it on only records where everyone stands, so it never alerts about old moves.
- **Weekly Team Recap**: from Monday 9:00, one notification per team about the week that just ended: your rank (and how it moved), your spend, who led, and your top model.
- **Team Challenges**: who won, when a challenge ends. Challenges that had already ended when you turned it on are never announced.
- **Team Reactions**: when a teammate gives you 🔥, 👏, or 🤡, one notification per team listing who gave what. Each reaction is announced once per day, even if it's taken back and given again. Reactions you already had when you turned it on are never announced.
- **Team Plan Changes** (owners only): when a covered member's Mac detects a different plan than the one a team plan is linked to (see **Plans**). Once per member and detected plan; mismatches already there when you turn it on are never announced.

- **Pace Alerts**, for the plans that cover you, only during your own working hours, and at most once a day per plan for each kind (both on by default):
  - **Slow Down**: at the current pace, today's budget runs out at least 30 minutes before your working day ends.
  - **Room to Spare**: from the middle of your working day, with at least an hour left: at the current pace, 30% or more of today's budget (and at least 3% of the weekly limit) goes unused. On the last working day before the reset, when what's left is lost rather than moved to the next days, 15% is enough, and the alert says so.

All six are checked after each upload, so after a refresh that changed your usage or at least every 15 minutes, and only while GodUsage is running. For Pace Alerts, an upload reloads your teams' plans at most once a minute.

## Requirements

Sign in goes through Apple's web sign-in, so it works in every build, including Developer ID releases (their provisioning profiles never grant the native Sign in with Apple entitlement). It needs the teams backend's Services IDs; see [Teams backend](teams-backend.md#sign-in-with-apple).
